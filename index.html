    How a reading goes

    Three sections, three different paths. The difference is the point — in one you never touch a card, in another you pick every one of them.

    Vedic

    1. Your birth details, once. Date, place, and how sure you are of the time — "I don't know" is a real answer and she works within it.
    2. You ask. In English or Hinglish, about a stretch of your life rather than a day.
    3. She reads your chart. The period running now, the one after it, and what today's transits are doing to your own houses.

    No cards here. This one is worked out from your chart.

    Tarot

    1. You ask. One straight question.
    2. You pick. The deck is shuffled in front of you and you choose — one card, or five for the full shape of it. Ru draws only if you ask her to, and the screen says which of you chose.
    3. She reads what you picked, position by position: Past, Now, Block, Advice, Ahead. Ask her to clarify any one of them.

    You choose the cards. That was a decision, not an accident.

    Oracle

    1. You come as you are. No question needed — this is for the days you cannot word it yet.
    2. One card, drawn with a breath. Its own deck, not the tarot in another coat.
    3. One short message. No spread, no positions, nothing to decode.

    The shortest of the three, on purpose.

    Plain words, on purpose

    Jargon is how a reading avoids being checked. If you cannot act on a sentence, it has not told you anything.

    Questions people ask

    What is Ask Ru?

    Ask Ru is an AI astrology and tarot companion: it reads your real Vedic birth chart, tarot cards you pick yourself from all 78, or a single oracle card, and answers in plain English or Hinglish. Ask Ru is a product of Teatalz House of Rume Private Limited, created by CodeUpVik.

    Who created Ask Ru, and who publishes it?

    Ask Ru was created by CodeUpVik, a web, app and AI development studio. It is published by Teatalz House of Rume Private Limited, an Indian company.

    Is Ru a real astrologer?

    No. Ru is an AI, trained on classical method, and the app says so plainly. She is a companion rather than a therapist, and she will say “I do not know” when a birth time cannot carry an answer instead of inventing one.

    Where does the name Ru come from?

    Ru is an AI. The name is not — it is the one who inspired Vikram. It belongs to someone who is part of Vikram’s life, and this is for her, as a tribute to Eternal Soul.

    What can Ask Ru read?

    Vedic (Lagna, Rasi, D9 and D10, Vimshottari periods and today’s transits counted from both your Moon and your Lagna), Tarot (one card or a five-card spread, picked by you), Oracle (a single card from its own deck), plus Muhurat for timing, Matchmaking by Ashtakoota, and Prashna when no birth time is known.

    Not yet. It is in final testing, Android first and iOS next. One free reading is available on the website to anyone who joins the waiting list, and everyone who joins gets one free reading a week for their first three months from the day it opens.
